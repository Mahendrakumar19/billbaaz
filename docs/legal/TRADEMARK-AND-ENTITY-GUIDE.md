# BillBaaz: Trademark Filing, Corporate Entity & IP Protection Guide

This guide details the practical legal and operational steps to secure the brand name **BillBaaz** ("Billing Ka Baaz! / Billing ka baazigar") as an intellectual property asset in India and incorporate your operating business entity.

---

## 1. Trademark Registration (IP India)

To prevent competitors (or former clients) from copying your brand name, logo, or tagline, file trademark applications with the **Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (CGPDTM)**.

### Trademark Classes to Register
File in multiple classes under the NICE classification system:

| Class | Description | Coverage for BillBaaz |
|---|---|---|
| **Class 9** | Computer software, POS hardware, downloadable apps, electronic registers | Point of Sale software, desktop MSIX client, mobile waiter apps, receipt generation software, pre-installed mini-PC appliances. |
| **Class 42** | Software as a Service (SaaS), IT consulting, cloud computing | Cloud restaurant dashboard, remote backup services, online menu hosting, QR ordering platform. |
| **Class 35** | Business management assistance, commercial administration | Restaurant back-office management systems, inventory tracking, payroll and staff management software. |

### Application Strategy
1. **Word Mark:** File a word mark application for the plain text **"BILLBAAZ"**. This protects the phonetic name regardless of font, color, or design styling.
2. **Device / Logo Mark:** File a separate device mark application containing your exact uploaded logo (the Falcon "B" speed emblem + "BillBaaz" typography + "Billing Ka Baaz!" tagline).
3. **Filing Portal:** File online via [ipindiaonline.gov.in](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in) (Standard official filing fee: ₹4,500 for Individual/Startup/MSME, or ₹9,000 for standard corporate). Once filed, you can legally affix the **™** symbol (`BillBaaz™`). After formal examination and registration, you switch to the **®** symbol.

## 3. Corporate Entity Incorporation (India)

To issue official GST tax invoices, sign corporate restaurant franchise contracts, and collect recurring SaaS subscription fees:

### Recommended Entity: Private Limited Company (Pvt. Ltd.)
* **Why:**
  - Limited liability protection (safeguards personal assets from business liabilities).
  - High corporate credibility when selling to major multi-outlet restaurant chains and franchises.
  - Required structure if you ever raise angel or venture capital funding.
  - Can register as an official DPIIT Recognized Startup under the *Startup India* program (giving 3 years of income tax exemption and 80% rebate on patent/trademark filing fees).
* **Alternative (Solo Founder / Low Budget Initial Launch):**
  - **One Person Company (OPC)** or **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)**.

## 4. Tax & Commercial Billing Setup (GST & Invoicing)

1. **GST Registration:**
   * **SAC Code:** `998314` (Information technology software design and development services / IT Consulting).
   * **Tax Rate:** 18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9% for intra-state sales; IGST 18% for inter-state sales).
2. **Current Bank Account:**
   * Open in the corporate name (e.g., *BillBaaz Technologies Private Limited*).
3. **Recurring Payment Gateway for SaaS Subscriptions:**
   * Apply for **Razorpay Subscriptions** or **Cashfree Subscriptions** to automate monthly recurring debit mandates (e-mandate / UPI Autopay / Card recurring) from your client restaurants.
